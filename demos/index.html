What is and is not happening here

Leo is invented. Every name, goal, food and appointment on these pages is synthetic, written to show the format rather than to describe a real child. These pages load no trackers and make no network calls once open. Your own version runs entirely on your own device, using your own model, and your family's folder never leaves it unless you send it somewhere yourself.
