Lesson chunks

The same task, in steps small enough to start.

A task comes home as one paragraph. For a lot of young people that paragraph is the wall. This is the identical work, reshaped so the first step is obvious and the end is visible.

What came home

Prepare a one page report on a dinosaur of your choice, including its habitat, diet, size, and three interesting facts, with a labelled diagram, to be presented to the class on Friday. Year 4 science, as issued

What Leo got

  1. Pick your dinosaur. Just the name.2 minutes
  2. Find one photo of it and save it.5 minutes
  3. Write three words about its size and where it lived.5 minutes
  4. Write what it ate. One sentence is enough.5 minutes
  5. Copy the three best facts from the book, not from memory.10 minutes
  6. Read it out loud once to someone. Then stop for today.5 minutes
0 of 6 done

Why this is not just a prettier list

The wall is usually the size of the first step, not the size of the task. Each chunk here has a visible finish and a time estimate, so starting is a smaller decision. The last step is deliberately a stop, which is part of the point.
