What I do

Four kinds of work, one rule throughout: the technology serves the people, never the other way round.

Custom agents, built for real work

The same craft behind the agents on this site. A personal AI assistant set up on your own machine, taught your routines, your tone and your boundaries. Local-first, so the work stays yours.

Workshops and team coaching

Your staff, volunteers or teaching team, from cautious to confident in a day. What AI is good at, where it quietly fails, what should never leave the room, and how to keep a person at the centre of every decision it touches.

Family setup sessions

One calm hour at the kitchen table: the agent installed, the parents briefed, the first routines running before bedtime. Built with particular care for neurodivergent families, where the right tool at the right moment can change the shape of a day.

Advice and strategy

Choosing tools, setting guardrails, writing the policy, briefing the board. Honest counsel on what to adopt now, what to leave alone for another year, and how to do this without either fear or hype.
